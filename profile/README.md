# BMLA - Bicocca Machine Learning Association

BMLA is a student-led Machine Learning community at the University of Milano-Bicocca.  

We bring together students passionate about AI, Machine Learning and Data Science through collaborative projects, reading groups, technical events and talks with researchers and industry professionals.  

Our mission is to foster a collaborative environment where students can learn together, exchange ideas and build meaningful real-world experience beyond the classroom.  

### 🚀 What we do

We organize and support activities such as:  
🧠 **Talks & seminars**: discussions with researchers, professionals, and students.  
💻 **Workshops & technical sessions**: hands-on sessions on ML, AI, Python, statistics, and related technologies.  
🛠️ **Projects**: collaborative projects designed to turn theoretical knowledge into practical experience.    
🤝 **Networking**: opportunities to connect with other students, academics, and professionals working in the field.  

### 👥 Who can join?

Everyone interested in Machine Learning, AI, Data Science, Statistics, or related fields is welcome.
You don't need to be an expert or have extensive experience. Whether you're just starting out or already working on advanced ML projects, BMLA is a place to learn and contribute.
